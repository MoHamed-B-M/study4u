# Project Brief: stdy4u

## Overview
**stdy4u** is a premium, high-fidelity student academic tracking and productivity mobile application. Designed with a "Material 3 Expressive" aesthetic, it combines academic management with motivational focus tools to help students stay on top of their studies in a fresh, modern environment.

## Target Audience
- University and high school students.
- Academic high-achievers who value data-driven insights.
- Students looking for an all-in-one hub for attendance, grades, and focus habits.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Clarity**: High-level overview of daily schedules and tasks.
- **Accountability**: Visual attendance tracking with threshold alerts.
- **Insight**: Deep-dive statistics into CGPA, grade distribution, and focus trends.
- **Aesthetic**: A "vibrant yet calming" UI that reduces academic stress through soft mint and lime-green palettes.

## Key Features

### 1. Dynamic Home (Dashboard)
- **Personalized Greeting**: Context-aware greeting (e.g., "Hello, Tareq! 👋").
- **Up Next**: High-priority card for the immediate next class or lab, including room number and time.
- **Course Management**: Quick access to all active courses (e.g., Physics, Logic Design, Data Structures).
- **Task Integration**: Integrated "Due Tasks" section with urgency indicators (Urgent/Normal).

### 2. Academic Tracker
- **Attendance Analytics**: Circular progress visualization of total attendance with threshold logic (e.g., "Above 75%").
- **Status Chips**: Categorized count of Present, Absent, and Late sessions.
- **Interactive Calendar**: Full monthly view for navigating daily schedules and historical attendance data.
- **Detailed Schedule**: Chronological list of daily classes with status tagging.

### 3. Statistics & Performance
- **Academic Metrics**: Real-time CGPA tracking with semester-over-semester evolution.
- **Focus Tools**: Integrated Pomodoro timer with "Focus" status and daily session counts.
- **Grade Distribution**: Visual bar chart showing grade spread (A+ to F).
- **Performance Overview**: Subject-specific progress bars with percentage and letter grade mappings.
- **Weekly Trends**: Line graph visualization of focus habits over time.

## Visual Identity
- **Design System**: Material 3 Expressive.
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Primary: Lime Green (#4ADE80).
  - Accents: Soft Mint, Warm Neutrals.
  - Background: Clean Surface (#F9F9FF) with lavender/purple gradient mockups.
- **Typography**: Outfit (Modern, friendly sans-serif).
- **Shapes**: Extra-large rounded corners (28-32px), soft shadows, and high-depth elevation.

## Success Metrics
- **Retention**: Daily active use for schedule checking and focus timing.
- **Achievement**: Improvement in focus session duration and grade awareness.
- **Adherence**: Reduction in "Absent" status through threshold reminders.
